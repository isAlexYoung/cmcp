# Third-party notices

## CMCP icon

`app/assets/cmcp.ico`, `app/assets/cmcp-icon-1024.png` and `app/assets/cmcp-icon.svg` are original artwork drawn for CMCP by `tools/draw-cmcp-icon.py`. They contain no Anthropic or Claude Desktop artwork. Earlier releases (up to 4.7.2) bundled the Claude Desktop icon; it is no longer included.

## Node.js

Portable releases bundle the official Node.js Windows x64 runtime. Its license and third-party notices are included at `app/runtime/LICENSE`. The source repository omits `node.exe`; development and CI install or provide the runtime separately.

## JavaScript dependencies

Direct dependencies are pinned in `app/package.json` and `app/package-lock.json`: `@modelcontextprotocol/sdk`, `@modelcontextprotocol/server-filesystem`, `express`, and `ws`. Their own license files and the licenses of included transitive packages are retained in the release's `app/node_modules` tree.
